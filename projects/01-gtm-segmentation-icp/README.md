# GTM Segmentation & ICP Scoring Engine

We were selling into every industry that would take a meeting, and win rates had
plateaued while pipeline kept growing. Everyone had a theory about which segments were
worth the effort, and every theory was somebody's anecdote about a deal they had worked.

I built this to settle it from the closed-won data instead. It reads the deal history,
measures which segments actually convert, and scores the open list so sales knows what
to work first. Realigning outbound and marketing content around what it found lifted
lead-to-opportunity conversion 12% over the following two quarters.

Rebuilt here as a standalone tool running on synthetic data.

## The problem it was solving

A company selling into every industry it can reach has no ICP — it has a list. Effort
spreads evenly across segments that convert at wildly different rates, marketing writes
for everyone, and win rate plateaus even while pipeline grows. The fix is not opinion.
It is looking at what has already closed.

## What it does

**`analyze`** reads 24 months of closed-won and closed-lost deals and measures win
rate, median deal cycle and won ARR across four dimensions — industry, company size
band, lead source, region. It converts each segment's win rate into a **lift** against
the company-wide baseline, and refuses to credit any segment with fewer than 12 deals
behind it (`MIN_SAMPLE`), because a 2-for-2 vertical is noise, not a market.

**`score`** applies that profile to open accounts. Each account gets a 0–100 fit score,
an A/B/C/D tier, a recommended action, and a one-line reason sales can read. Points
are allocated 45 / 25 / 20 / 10 across industry, size, source and region — industry
weighted heaviest because it carried by far the widest win-rate spread — and each
dimension is normalised against its own observed lift range, so the best-converting
segment earns full marks and the score stays readable.

Nothing here is a black box: the weights are derived from the deal history, written
to `out/icp_profile.json`, and a sales leader can audit every number.

## Run it

```bash
python3 src/icp_engine.py analyze     # segment the history, write the ICP profile
python3 src/icp_engine.py score       # score open accounts against that profile
```

No dependencies. Python 3.8+. Point `--deals` / `--accounts` at your own CSVs to
run it on real data.

## What it found in the sample data

```
Baseline win rate 38.8% | median won-deal cycle 90 days | $13.1M won ARR

INDUSTRY        DEALS WIN RATE    LIFT    CYCLE    WON ARR
  Healthcare       80    55.0%   1.42x     83 d      $4.6M
  Banking          42    54.8%   1.41x     84 d      $2.0M
  Pharmaceutics    34    50.0%   1.29x     77 d      $1.7M
  Education        41    41.5%   1.07x    102 d      $1.1M
  ...
  Hospitality      32    15.6%   0.40x    115 d      $174K

ICP CANDIDATE: Healthcare, Banking, Pharmaceuticals
  64.1% of won ARR from 37.1% of pipeline volume
  median cycle 81 days vs 90 overall (8 days shorter)
```

Three regulated verticals produce **64% of won ARR from 37% of deal volume**, and
close eight days faster. The shared driver is compliance urgency — these buyers have
a deadline someone else set for them. That is the ICP.

Applied to 75 open accounts, 34 of them (45%) land in tiers A and B, covering 50% of
estimated ARR. The other half of the list stops consuming outbound capacity.

## Where it plugs in

| Step | Tool | How |
|---|---|---|
| Deal history in | Salesforce | Scheduled report export, or SOQL against `Opportunity` |
| Account enrichment | Clay | Firmographic + regulatory-status enrichment before scoring |
| Scores out | HubSpot | `out/hubspot_batch_update.json` → `POST /crm/v3/objects/companies/batch/update` |
| Orchestration | Zapier | See [`automation/zapier-recipe.md`](automation/zapier-recipe.md) |
| Review | Claude | [`.claude/skills/gtm-segmentation`](.claude/skills/gtm-segmentation/SKILL.md) |

## Outputs

- `out/icp_profile.json` — the derived weights, auditable
- `out/scored_accounts.csv` — every account with score, tier, action and reason
- `out/hubspot_batch_update.json` — CRM write-back payload
